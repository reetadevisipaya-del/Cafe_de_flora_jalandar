✦ Discover Café Dé Floraa

A little escape from the everyday.

Café Dé Floraa was born out of a desire for warmth, gentle conversations, and vibrant culinary craft. We are not a dark, hurried coffee shop — here, lush botanical greens meet soothing ivory tones, delicate florals adorn the tables, and the aroma of freshly pulled 9-bar espresso welcomes you into a tranquil garden sanctuary.

Whether you're sharing a steaming plate of Aglio e Olio, biting into our molten Cheese Mushroom Toast, or sipping on a handcrafted Lotus Biscoff Shake, every single moment here is crafted to make you slow down and smile.
